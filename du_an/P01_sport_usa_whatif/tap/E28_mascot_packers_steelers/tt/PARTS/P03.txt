Round two. Defense.
Pittsburgh drops an actual Steel Curtain.
Fans named that defense too. Radio contest, nineteen seventy-one.
Sausages bounce off. Cheese bounces off. Forklift? Bounces off.
But Green Bay has one move nobody else in the league has.
The fans own the team. Shareholders since nineteen twenty-three.
And owners vote.
Motion to scrap the curtain. All in favor?
Every cheesehead goes up.
Motion passed. The Steel Curtain gets sold for scrap.
