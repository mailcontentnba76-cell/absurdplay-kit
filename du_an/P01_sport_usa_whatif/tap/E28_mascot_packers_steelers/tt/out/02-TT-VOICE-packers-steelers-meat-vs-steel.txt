What if the Packers were actual meat packers... | and the Steelers were actual steelworkers? | In Green Bay, jerseys rip off. Hairnets go on. | Fun fact. The Packers are named after a meat packing company. | In nineteen nineteen, it paid for their first jerseys. | So Green Bay is just going back to work. | Now Pittsburgh. Hard hats. Molten steel, over two thousand degrees. | And they started as the Pittsburgh Pirates. | In nineteen forty, fans renamed them in a newspaper contest. | Pirates became Steelers. Arrr became clang. | One field. One football. Zero rules. | Round one. Trophies. | Pittsburgh rolls out six Super Bowl wins. Tied for the most ever. | Green Bay answers with a forklift. Thirteen league titles. Most in NFL history. | Round two. Defense. | Pittsburgh drops an actual Steel Curtain. | Fans named that defense too. Radio contest, nineteen seventy-one. | Sausages bounce off. Cheese bounces off. Forklift? Bounces off. | But Green Bay has one move nobody else in the league has. | The fans own the team. Shareholders since nineteen twenty-three. | And owners vote. | Motion to scrap the curtain. All in favor? | Every cheesehead goes up. | Motion passed. The Steel Curtain gets sold for scrap. | Pittsburgh fires back. Every fan whips out a Terrible Towel. | A radio guy invented it in nineteen seventy-five. | Green Bay answers with the cheesehead. | Invented in nineteen eighty-seven... out of a couch cushion. | So Pittsburgh fans name everything. Green Bay fans own everything. | Steel or sausage? Comment who wins.
