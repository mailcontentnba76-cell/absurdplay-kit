What the heck? Could the Philadelphia 76ers beat 50 babies in a playoff game?
Philly started nine and two. They have Tyrese Maxey, Jaylen Brown, and LeBron James. The babies have zero NBA experience. Their starting center can't even stand up.
This should be the easiest win in playoff history. Except it's the playoffs.
Philadelphia hasn't reached the conference finals since 2001. Last spring, the Knicks swept them in the second round. The babies, meanwhile, are undefeated in the postseason.
Tip-off. A baby grabs Maxey's shoelace. Maxey trips. Babies' ball.
LeBron drives the lane. Forty babies are sitting in the paint. They've been there all quarter. The refs call nothing.
Jaylen Brown is wide open from three. A baby starts crying. Brown stops to check on him. Shot clock violation.
At halftime, it's zero to zero. The broadcast calls it a gritty defensive battle.
Fourth quarter. Eight seconds left. Maxey crosses over and steps back... right onto a pacifier.
The ball rolls into the baby pile. Nobody can find it.
One baby throws it over his head. Off the glass. In.
Final score, babies two, Sixers zero. The babies advance. Philadelphia goes home.
After the game, Philly says the babies had an easy schedule.
