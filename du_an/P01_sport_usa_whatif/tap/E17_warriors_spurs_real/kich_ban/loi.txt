Fourteen. That's how many points the Warriors scored in the first quarter. Let's rewind.

Friday night, San Antonio. The Spurs host the Warriors. One simple question: could Golden State score fifteen points in a quarter?

Tip-off. The Spurs come in on a ten-game winning streak. The Warriors come in... hoping.

First quarter ends: Spurs 38, Warriors 14. So, no. Fifteen was too much.

Second quarter: Golden State scores nineteen. Progress! Halftime: 63–33.

Meanwhile, Stephon Castle is doing whatever he wants. Thirty-two points. Twelve of eighteen. Five threes.

Victor Wembanyama? Twelve points, twelve rebounds, six assists. He only needed twenty-one minutes.

At one point, the Spurs lead by forty-three. Forty-three! Even the scoreboard looks tired.

The Warriors' top scorer? L.J. Cryer, twenty points off the bench. Everybody else? Eleven or less.

Final: Spurs 123, Warriors 85. San Antonio wins eleven straight. Then they win again on Sunday. Twelve and two.

And Golden State? Fourteen in a quarter. Next time, the goal is fifteen. You can do it.
