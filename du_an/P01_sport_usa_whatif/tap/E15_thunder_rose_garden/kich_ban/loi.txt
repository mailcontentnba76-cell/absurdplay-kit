What the heck?! Could the Thunder beat the Blazers if the game was played in a giant rose garden?
On Tuesday, Portland beat Oklahoma City 123 to 103. On Wednesday, they played again. Portland won again, 118 to 108. Two nights, two losses.
So OKC asks for one small change. Portland is the Rose City, so the rematch moves into the biggest rose garden in Oregon.
Tip-off. The court is covered in petals. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander still scores, he had 31 last night.
But the Blazers know every path in this garden. Deni Avdija cuts through the tulips. He had 28.
OKC tries to guard him with a hedge. Okay, Thunder bench, plan B. A giant lawnmower to clear the court. It mows a perfect path, straight to the Portland basket.
Fourth quarter. OKC actually takes the lead. Then Damian Lillard walks out of a rose bush. Avdija and Lillard score 20 of Portland's 30 in the quarter.
Final: Portland 118, OKC 108. Same as last night.
Turns out, the Blazers are great at gardening. They're 8 and 3. And the Thunder leave Oregon with zero wins and petals in their shoes.
Next time, OKC wants a cactus garden.
