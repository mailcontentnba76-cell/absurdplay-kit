What the fuck? Could the four worst teams in the NBA beat five traffic cones?
Except, combined, the Bucks, Kings, Jazz and Bulls are four and thirty-five.
Utah has lost eight straight. Chicago, seven. Milwaukee, six.
And one of their four wins? The Bulls beat the Bucks. They had to play each other to get one.
So the league merges them into one superteam. Twenty players. Four coaches. One goal.
Tip-off. The cone doesn't jump. Utah still loses the tip.

Sacramento hits it with a hesi. The cone doesn't bite. The cone didn't move.

Fourth quarter. Still zero to zero. Four seconds left.
Utah grabs the rebound, spins, and lays it in... at the wrong basket.
Final score, Cones two, Worst Four zero.
