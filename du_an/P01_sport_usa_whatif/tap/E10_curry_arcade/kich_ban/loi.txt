What the fuck? Could thirty-eight-year-old Steph Curry beat five arcade basketball machines?
Except these machines have never missed. Not once. They were literally built to make shots.
Saturday night, Curry dropped forty-one on Orlando. Thirteen of nineteen. Ten for ten from the line. In overtime.
And Golden State started oh and three, then won seven straight.
So the Warriors drive him to a boardwalk arcade. One token. One rack. Sixty seconds.
The buzzer goes. The machines start firing. Swish. Swish. Swish.
Curry pulls up from the parking lot. Swish.
The machine answers. Swish. It doesn't even blink. It doesn't have eyes.
Midway through, the rim starts shrinking. Classic carnival. It's an oval now.
Curry adjusts. Off the glass. Off the rim. Off a seagull. Still counts.
Ten seconds left. Down one. The machines never get tired.
Curry fires from behind the claw machine. The lights go crazy. Tickets everywhere.
Final score, Curry ninety-eight, machines ninety-seven.
His prize? Four thousand tickets. Enough for one plastic whistle.
Back in Orlando, the Magic install five machines at practice. They still lose in overtime.
