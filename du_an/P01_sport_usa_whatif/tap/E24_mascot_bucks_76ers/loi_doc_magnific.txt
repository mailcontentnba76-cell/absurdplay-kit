[energetic, fast-paced narrator] What if every NBA team was its actual mascot? The Bulls? Actual bulls. The Rockets? Actual rockets. The Suns? The actual sun. Good luck.

This weekend. Game one. The Bucks? Real deer. The Sixers? Real Philadelphians from seventeen seventy-six. Wigs, three-cornered hats, the whole Declaration.

On paper, it's a massacre. In real life, Milwaukee is one and fourteen. Worst start in team history. Philly? Thirteen and three. First in the East.

Tip-off. Fun fact: a buck can clear a nine-foot fence. The rim is ten feet. So every dunk... one foot short. Clang. Clang.

Sixers ball. They don't run plays. They draft them. On parchment. With a quill. Shot clock violation.

So the Bucks use the one thing they've got. Speed. Forty miles an hour. Coast to coast. Bucks lead, eight to two.

Then Philly rings the Liberty Bell. Deer flash their white tails when they're scared. Five white flags. Five bucks off the court. Sixers, twenty to eight.

Here's the thing about bucks. They lose their antlers every single year. This summer, Milwaukee lost a six-foot-eleven Greek one.

Fourth quarter. Philly rings the bell again... Crack. It's the Liberty Bell. Of course it cracked. No sound. No scared deer. Bucks storm back. Down one. Last shot.

One buck takes off from the free-throw line... nine feet... he needs ten... The Sixers unroll the Declaration. "All men are created equal." The ref looks at the deer. "You're not men."

That's Saturday in Philly. Antlers, or the Declaration? Comment who wins.
