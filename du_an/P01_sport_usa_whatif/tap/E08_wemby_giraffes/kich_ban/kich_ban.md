## Lời (đọc bằng giọng Sport USA)
What the f***? Could Victor Wembanyama beat five giraffes?
Except these giraffes are the only players on Earth who look down at him.
On Wednesday, Wemby dropped fifty-two points, twenty-four rebounds and eight blocks on Dallas. Eight.
He went one for eight from three, and still finished plus twenty-three in a one-point game.
So the Spurs fly him to the savanna for a real test. Tip-off.
Wemby wins the jump ball. The giraffe eats it.
Pick-and-roll. The giraffes switch everything. Wemby rises for his fadeaway. Blocked. By a neck.
He tries a lob to himself. The giraffe eats it.
Drop coverage? They don't drop. They're already up there.
At halftime, the studio crew calls it the greatest defense in NBA history. The defense is chewing a tree.
Fourth quarter. Down one. Eight seconds left.
Wemby drives baseline, rises, and dunks on a giraffe who fell asleep standing up. And-one.
He walks to the line. Nineteen for twenty-three on Wednesday. Ice cold. Swish.
Final score, Wemby fifty-two, Giraffes fifty-one.
After the game, the Mavericks sign two giraffes. They still lose by one.
