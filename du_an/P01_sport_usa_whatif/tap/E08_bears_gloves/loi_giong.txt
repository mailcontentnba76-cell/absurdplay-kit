What the fuck? Caleb Williams threw four interceptions... and the Bears still won. So this week, the Bears play eleven baseball gloves. Why gloves? Because if Caleb's going to throw it to the other team anyway... they might as well be good at catching. Last night: four picks, five turnovers, and a win over Tampa Bay on a field goal with no time left. The Bears are seven and two. Kickoff on a sandlot at sunset. Caleb Williams, number eighteen, drops back, throws over the middle... Caught. By a glove. Next drive. Deep shot down the sideline... Caught. Screen pass... Caught. A handoff... somehow, caught. On the broadcast, the analyst says, "He's just not seeing the gloves." His partner says, "They're the size of a car." Fourth quarter. Caleb has thrown eleven interceptions. One to every glove. Fair is fair. Last play. Cairo Santos lines up a thirty-eight yarder. All eleven gloves jump to catch it... but gloves can't jump. They don't have legs. It's good! Bears win, twenty-seven to twenty-four. Somehow... again. After the game, the gloves asked Caleb for an autograph. He threw it to them. They caught it.
