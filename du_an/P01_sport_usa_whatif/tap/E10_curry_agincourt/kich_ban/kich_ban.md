## Lời
What the fuck? Could thirty-eight-year-old Steph Curry outshoot the English archers at the Battle of Agincourt?
Except these guys were the deadliest shooters of the Middle Ages.
Saturday night, Curry dropped forty-one on Orlando. Thirteen of nineteen. Ten for ten from the line. In overtime.
He's also the only player in NBA history with four thousand made threes.
Meanwhile, October twenty-fifth, fourteen fifteen. A muddy field in northern France. Thousands of English longbowmen, each firing about ten arrows a minute.
So Curry time-travels to the front line. One ball. One hoop. No shot clock.
The archers fire the first volley. The sky goes dark.
Curry pulls up from two hundred yards. Splash. Night night.
The French knights charge through the mud. Curry hits a step-back over a horse. And-one.
The archers adjust. Volley after volley. Curry answers every one. Splash. Night night.
At halftime, two monks on the studio desk call it the greatest shooting display of the fifteenth century.
Final volley. Curry slips in the mud, falls backwards, and banks it in anyway.
Final score, Curry two hundred thirteen, archers zero. Arrows don't count without a hoop.
King Henry knights him on the spot. Sir Stephen still has to be home by tip-off.
