01. The Social Media Oversharer

Everyone knows that one guy that treats social media like his personal therapist. In real life, he’s chill, laughing, acting like nothing bothers him. But the moment he gets on Instagram, he starts having a whole emotional documentary on his story.

He’ll post a black screen with a broken heart like he just lived through three seasons of a drama show. Sad R&B song playing in the background, text saying, “Can’t trust nobody no more.” Sir, someone took your seat in the cafeteria. Calm down.

He’ll be on Snapchat like, “I’m done with everybody,” then post again four minutes later like, “Nobody understands me.” Yes, because you’re speaking in riddles. He always types like he’s going through a spiritual awakening. “Some people don’t deserve my energy.” Bro, the only energy you have is the battery life on your phone.

But the funniest part? In person, he’s totally fine. Smiling, laughing, acting like he didn’t post a whole emotional breakdown at 2:00 a.m. Social media sees his pain. Reality sees him eating Hot Cheetos in peace. He’s not mysterious. He’s just dramatic.


02. The Self-Proclaimed Player

Everyone knows that one guy at school who swears he’s a player. He’s always standing there with one leg propped up like he’s posing for a shoe commercial. Head tilted, arms crossed, chewing gum like he’s in a music video. He really thinks every hallway is his runway.

And he loves taking selfies. He’ll catch himself in the front camera and suddenly forget where he is. Fixing his hair, adjusting his eyebrows, doing that fake jawline push-out like it’s gonna magically grow. Every photo he posts is a thirst trap nobody asked for.

He can’t be around too many guys either. The moment he sees a dude-to-girl ratio above three to one, he starts complaining. “Ah, bro, this is a straight sausage fest. I can’t be here.” Sir, you are contributing to the ratio. Calm down.

The second he sees girls walking by, he switches personalities instantly. Suddenly he’s leaning more, talking deeper, pretending he’s mysterious, laughing at jokes he didn’t hear, acting like he’s the prize of the whole school. He’ll say stuff like, “Girls stay choosing me, bro.” Meanwhile, the only thing choosing him is the Wi-Fi network.


03. The Friend with an Attitude

Everyone knows that one girl who always has an attitude. Like, she just wakes up mad at the world for no reason. You can walk up all friendly like, “Hey, how’s your day?” and she’ll look at you like you just offended her entire family.

She’s constantly making those ugly little faces. The eye roll, the lip curl, the deep sigh, like everything you say is an inconvenience. You ask her one normal question and she snaps back with, “Why you asking me?” She acts like she’s fighting invisible battles all day.

Teachers ask her to take out her AirPods, and she gives them the same look a villain gives before destroying a city. And the funniest part? She’s mad at everything except snacks. You hand her a bag of something she likes, and suddenly she’s smiling like nothing ever happened. Mood swings unlocked like DLC.

She’ll be rude all morning, roast everybody in the hallway, act annoyed at the world. But the minute she starts munching, she turns normal again, like someone flipped her switch. She’s got attitude for no reason, faces for no reason, beef with the air for no reason. But somehow every friend group has at least one of her.


04. The Self-Centered Drama Queen

Everyone knows that one girl who swears she’s the main character of the entire school. She walks the halls like it’s her personal red carpet. Like everyone’s supposed to stop and admire her entrance.

And when she doesn’t get what she wants, she makes the meanest faces. Like, full villain mode. She’ll look at you like you ruined her life over something tiny. She also swears every guy is after her. A dude asks what page the homework is on, and she’s already like, “He’s totally into me.” A guy says “Excuse me” while passing. “He’s obsessed.” Girl, he was trying to get around you because you walk one mile per hour.

But here’s the best part. She will talk so much behind your back. Full gossip, full drama, telling people she can’t stand you. But the moment you confront her, she switches up immediately. She’ll hit you with, “O-M-G, what? No, I—I—I would never say that,” then smile like she’s innocent and has no idea what’s going on.

She lies so fast she should major in politics. She thinks she’s the prize, the star, the celebrity of the school.


05. The Imaginary Heartbreaker

Everybody knows that one guy who swears he pulls every girl on Earth, but nobody has ever seen him talk to a single woman in real life. He’ll walk in the room like, “Bro, I got hella girls.” Sir, the only females you talk to are the cashiers at Chick-fil-A, and even they call you “buddy.”

He’ll be like, “My girl bad, but she goes to another school.” Yeah? Name it. He’ll start stuttering like, “Uh, Riverside.” That school don’t even exist, my boy.

He swears girls blow up his phone, but every time he opens his messages, it’s just a Snapchat memory from 2018 and a text from his mom saying, “Come.” He talks like he’s in a love triangle every week. “Bro, this one girl obsessed with me.” No, she’s not. She asked you for a pencil in seventh grade and you haven’t recovered.

He’ll post vague quotes like, “She knows who she is.” Yes, she is fictional. Your whole love life runs on imagination and free Wi-Fi. He truly believes he’s a heartbreaker. Meanwhile, the only thing he’s ever broken is his screen protector.


06. The Guy Who Thinks He’s Famous

Everyone knows that one guy who walks around school like he’s too famous to be there. He’ll stroll in with sunglasses on, indoors, looking left and right like paparazzi might pop out from behind the locker.

He’ll interrupt any conversation just to talk about himself. You could be talking about your homework, your dog, world news, and he’ll jump in like, “Bro, that reminds me of when I…” Sir, nobody asked. Nobody even looked at you.

He brags about everything, too. His shoes, his connections, his money, his fans. He’ll be like, “Yeah, people stay watching me.” No, they don’t. People stay avoiding you. He really thinks everyone likes him. He’ll walk through the hallway nodding at random people like he’s greeting paparazzi. But in reality, everyone has him muted in group chats.

And the funniest part? He calls himself the black sheep, like he’s so mysterious and misunderstood. Bro, you’re not the black sheep. You just have a personality people don’t want to babysit. He truly believes he’s the star of the school, but to everyone else, he’s just the background character who talks too much.


07. The Wannabe Gangster

Everyone knows that one kid who swears he’s a gangster. Like, he walks the school halls acting like he runs a whole cartel. Hood up, hands in his pockets, looking around like he’s checking for opps. Bro, you’re checking for hall monitors.

He’ll be like, “I don’t go class. That school stuff weak.” Yes, and so are your grades, my boy. He skips class like it’s a personality trait, sitting in the restroom thinking he’s in a music video. He throws fake gang signs at people he doesn’t know, talks in a voice that drops three octaves when teachers walk by, and always says stuff like, “Bro, I’m in the streets.” No, you’re not. You’re in algebra, seat 27B, facing the whiteboard.

And the best part? At the end of the day, after all that tough-guy energy, his mom pulls up in the golden minivan blasting Christian radio. He always steps in real slow, looking around to make sure nobody sees him. Then he goes all the way to the very back seat like he’s hiding from the FBI.

He’ll slam the door and mumble, “Drive, woman.” Meanwhile, she’s like, “Sweetie, do you want McDonald’s before we go home?” My guy thinks he’s a menace, but he’s really just a passenger in the third row.


08. The Know-It-All

Everyone knows that one guy who swears he’s the main character. He walks the halls like there’s dramatic theme music playing behind him. Slow-motion strut, like bro thinks he’s in a Netflix original. He genuinely believes the world revolves around him. Group conversation? He has to jump in. Doesn’t matter what the topic is. He’s suddenly the expert. He’ll interrupt with, “Actually, what really happened was…” Bro, we didn’t ask.

He’s the type who always has to be right, even when he’s loud and wrong. You could show him evidence, screenshots, a whole documentary, and he’ll still be like, “Nah, bro, trust me.”

He thinks he’s the prize, too. Talks to girls like he’s blessing them with his presence. He’ll say stuff like, “Yeah, she likes me. Everyone does.” Sir, the only thing that likes you is your reflection.

In class, he sits there with that dramatic “I’m too deep for this world” look, staring out the window like he’s reflecting on trauma he doesn’t even have. Teacher asks a question and his hand shoots up immediately, even when he doesn’t know the answer. He just wants to talk. He truly thinks he’s the star of the show.


09. The All-Talk Tough Guy

Everyone knows that one annoying kid who swears he’s tougher than everybody. But the second someone confronts him, he turns into a scared Roomba bumping into walls.

He’s always lying about some wild accident, too. Come to school with a tiny scratch and be like, “Yeah, I survived a bike crash. Three flips. Almost died. Doctor said I’m a miracle.” Bro, you walked into a locker. Stop.

He talks the most trash for no reason. He’ll be like, “If he says that again, I’m swinging.” But then the guy walks up and suddenly he’s shaking like an unstable washing machine. “Uh, wait. Chill. It was a joke.”

He brags about fights he’s never been in, gym numbers he’s never lifted, girls he’s never talked to. He’ll say, “Bro, you don’t want these hands.” He always tries to sound intimidating, too, lowering his voice like, “Yeah, bro, I’m not scared of nobody.” Meanwhile, when someone bumps into him on accident, he jumps like he’s being kidnapped.

And the lies never stop. “My dad trains me in secret combat.” No, he doesn’t. Your dad works at Costco. He’s a tough guy in his imagination, but in real life, he’s folding like a beach chair.


10. The Relentless Roaster

Everyone knows that one friend who can’t walk into a room without making fun of somebody. Like, the door opens and you already feel spiritually prepared to get roasted. He hasn’t even sat down yet and he’s already like, “Boy, what you wearing?”

He’ll clown anything. Your shoes, your haircut, your sneeze, your breathing pattern. Nothing is safe. You could be having the best day of your life and he’ll still find a way to humble you in three seconds.

And the worst part? He laughs so hard at his own jokes. Like, tears in his eyes, wheezing for air, slapping the table type laughter. He’s not even waiting for the joke to land. He’s the audience. He’ll joke about you being broke, short, tired, hungry, happy, sad. Doesn’t matter. This man treats life like an open mic comedy show that only he wanted to perform at.

But low-key, he’s kind of a bully. He’ll disguise it as, “Bro, I’m just playing,” but the way he laughs feels personal. He’ll roast you so bad you start questioning things you were confident about. And when you clap back, he suddenly becomes emotional. Like, “Dang, bro, chill on me. That’s crazy.”
