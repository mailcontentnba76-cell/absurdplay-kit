11. The Always-Hungry Friend

Everyone knows that one kid who just cannot stop eating. Like, he finishes his food in 30 seconds flat, sits back, looks around the table, and immediately hits you with, “Yo, you gonna eat that, bro?” Doesn’t matter if you just picked up your fork. He’s already scanning your plate like a hawk.

He’s the type to say he’s not hungry, then demolish half your fries the second you’re not looking. You bring snacks to school? He smells it from three classrooms away. He’ll appear out of nowhere like, “What you got there?”

At lunch, he eats his tray, your tray, and somehow still says, “I low-key could go for seconds, bro.” No, you are good. And the funniest part? He goes to sleep early on purpose just so breakfast can come faster. He treats tomorrow’s meal like Christmas morning. He’ll wake up like, “Yes, it’s time.”

He’s always hungry, always eating, always asking, and always trying to sample your food. He’s not just hungry. He’s permanently in meal mode. He’s not a friend. He’s a walking appetite with a backpack.


12. The Full-Time Flirt

Everyone knows that one kid who is a full-time flirt. Like, he talks to every girl in the school. DMs, texts, snaps, voice memos. His phone is basically a hotline at this point.

You can’t have one conversation with him without him bragging like, “Bro, guess who I was with last night.” It’s always a different name, too. He got a new situationship every 48 hours. He thinks he’s the smoothest man alive. Half the time he’s staring at his reflection, fixing his hair, biting his lip at his own front camera like he’s practicing for a music video.

Then he posts those thirst traps. Shirt lifted, jawline forced out, filters on max. Caption something like, “Don’t fall for me.” Bro, nobody is falling. They’re tripping, trying to get away. He’s always bragging, “Bro, these girls stay wanting me.” No, they stay blocking you.

He’ll claim he’s in love one day and single for life the next because he can’t go 12 hours without attention. He’s the type to flirt with three girls at lunch, text two more during fourth period, and still say, “Bro, I’m loyal.” Loyal to who? He’s not the main character. He’s the school’s walking red flag.


13. The Effortless Charmer

Everyone knows that one guy who is a real player. Not the wannabe, the actual problem. He gets all the girls, walks the halls like his presence is a rare item drop. Just aura farming everywhere he stands. He doesn’t even talk. He just exists, and girls start giggling.

And when you talk to him, oh, it’s over. He does that signature move. The slow eye squint, the lip bite, the little head tilt like he’s shooting a music video in his mind. You can’t even ask him, “Bro, what period you got next?” without him looking at you like he’s posing for a perfume commercial.

He’s always leaning on something, too. Lockers, walls, door frames, acting like gravity barely applies to him. Girls walk by and suddenly he straightens up, fixes his shirt, runs his hand through his hair, then drops the lip bite like a final attack move.

And the craziest part? It works. He’ll say the bare minimum, like “Hey,” and girls start blushing like he proposed. He’s not just a player. He’s the school’s final boss of rizz. Standing by the lockers, just aura farming.


14. The Habitual Liar

Everyone knows that one guy who lies for no reason at all. Like, he wakes up every morning and chooses dishonesty as his personality. He’ll lie about stuff nobody even asked about. You’ll be like, “Bro, where were you yesterday?” And he’ll say, “Oh, I was in Miami meeting celebrities.” Sir, we saw you in math class.

He lies for fun. Pure entertainment. You say you got a new pair of shoes, and suddenly he’s like, “Bro, I got the same ones, but in a limited edition that hasn’t even dropped yet.” No, you don’t. Your mom bought you Skechers.

He’ll tell stories like he lives in an action movie. “Yeah, bro, I almost got kidnapped last night, but I fought them off.” No, you didn’t. You were at Walmart with your grandma.

He lies so much that even he forgets what’s real. He’ll tell one story Monday, a different version Tuesday, and on Wednesday he’s shocked you remembered both. He stays coming up with new lies for no reason. He’s not trying to impress anyone. He just lies because he’s bored. He is the human version of “I made it up because it sounded better.”


15. Mr. Perfect

Everyone knows that one guy who swears he’s perfect. He’ll spend 20 minutes in the mirror before class, tilting his head, fixing his hair, checking his jawline, making sure he’s looksmaxxing to the absolute max. He’ll be like, “Bro, symmetry is everything.” Sir, your eyebrows disagree.

And he loves giving lectures. You take out one bag of chips and he’s instantly in your face like, “Bro, do you know what that does to your body? Your insulin spikes, your aura collapses, your gains disappear.” Relax, Professor. It’s a Dorito.

He always says the most unhinged things, too, like he thinks it makes him mysterious. He’ll randomly be like, “I don’t chase girls. They chase me spiritually.” Or, “My DNA hits different.” Bro, your DNA is fighting for its life.

He walks around school like he’s the blueprint for perfection. Fixing his shirt, adjusting his chain, checking every reflection he passes. But deep down, everyone knows he’s just normal. Not terrible, not great, just aggressively average. He thinks he’s flawless, but he’s really just a dude with too much confidence and too many mirrors.


16. The Wannabe Class Clown

Everyone knows that one kid who really thinks he’s the class clown. But he’s not funny. He’s just loud for no reason. He walks the halls every morning like he’s warming up for a comedy special.

He posts up by the lockers waiting for someone, anyone, to walk by so he can try one of his pranks. And the reaction is always the same. Just a blink. A calm blink. And he still laughs like he broke the internet.

In class, he’s even worse. He tries to crack jokes during serious moments, makes weird sound effects, and when nobody laughs, he doubles down like, “Y’all don’t get my humor.” Bro, there is no humor. Stop blaming us.

He really thinks he’s carrying the whole school on his back, like he’s the comedic glue holding everything together. He’ll even introduce himself with “I’m the funny one,” but nobody ever asked.

He wants to be the star so bad, but he’s more like the preview ad before the real movie. Loud, unnecessary, and nobody’s paying attention. He’s not the class clown. He’s the class cough. Random, annoying, and always showing up when you don’t want him to.


17. The Friend Who Takes Everything Personally

Everyone knows that one guy who gets butthurt over the smallest joke. Like, you can’t even tease him a little bit without him acting like you just destroyed his childhood. You’ll say something harmless like, “Bro, your backpack kinda small,” and he’ll go quiet, look down, and whisper, “Why would you say that?”

He’s too emotional. You’re literally joking around with the whole group, everybody laughing, having a good time. And the moment he thinks the joke is even slightly about him, his whole mood collapses. He starts staring at the floor like he’s in a sad music video.

He’s the type to say, “Wow, I didn’t know you felt that way,” over a joke that wasn’t even serious. Like, bro, calm down. Nobody wrote a hate speech. And the worst part? He remembers everything.

You’ll joke about his shoes once, and two weeks later he’s still bringing it up like it was a traumatic event. “Remember when you said my shoes were dusty?” No, bro, you need therapy, not a reminder.

He’s so sensitive you gotta double-check every sentence before you say it. You can’t laugh too loud. Can’t tease him even slightly or he’ll start acting like you betrayed him. He’s the walking definition of “I’m fine, but I’m not fine.”


18. The Overconfident Flirt

Everyone knows that one kid who is chopped. Like, painfully chopped. But he refuses to accept it. He looks in the mirror every morning hyping himself up like, “Nah, bro, I’m kinda fine.” Kinda? My boy, you are fighting for your life in 4K.

He always blames it on the lighting, too. “It’s just bad angles.” No, bro, that’s the factory settings. And he swears he pulls every girl. He’ll talk to one girl who only said “Move,” and he’ll walk away like, “Bro, she feeling me.” She wasn’t feeling you. She was trying to get around you.

He tries to spit game and it’s tragic. Walks up like, “Uh, you like stuff?” She hits him with “I gotta go,” and he tells his boys, “She couldn’t handle the pressure.” No, bro, she couldn’t handle the jump scare.

He posts selfies like he’s modeling. Chin up, hand on his face, squinting. But the comments be like, “You tried.” “Delete this.” And he still thinks he cooked.

He claims he has unlimited rizz, god-tier game, but deep down he knows he’s chopped. You see it when he avoids mirrors with honest lighting.


19. The Couple That Fights for Fun

Everyone knows that one toxic couple that treats every day like a new episode of a drama series. They start the morning off perfect. Compliments, hugs, matching lock screens. “Babe, you look so cute today.” “Babe, you’re my whole world.” It’s adorable for, like, 12 minutes.

Then out of nowhere, they switch up. One wrong word, one wrong look, and suddenly they’re arguing in front of the vending machine like the relationship is on trial. They can’t go one hour without beef. Not one.

You’ll be talking to your boy about something completely unrelated, like, “Bro, did you see that homework?” And his girl will instantly go silent, arms crossed, eyes squinting, acting like you just complimented another woman in front of her. She gets mad at words that weren’t even for her.

And she stays blowing up his phone. Back-to-back-to-back notifications. “Where you at?” “Why you not answering?” “Who you with?” He’ll be in the bathroom for two minutes and she’s already texting like he ran off to start a new life.

They break up every week and get back together before lunch ends. It’s not a relationship. It’s a full-time sport. They are the definition of “We love each other, but we also fight for fun.”


20. The Image-Obsessed Tough Guy

Everyone knows that one guy with the Edgar cut. He spends, like, half an hour in the mirror every morning just adjusting that haircut, brushing it forward, patting it down, making sure it’s perfectly straight. Bro treats his bangs like they’re national security.

And he always wears the same Air Force 1s. They haven’t been white since elementary school. They’re creased, dirty, the laces are gray, but he still walks around like he’s wearing designer.

He’s obsessed with Hot Cheetos, too. Fingers red, crumbs on his hoodie, bag hanging out his pocket like a prop. Every time he talks, you can see the dust flying. And of course, he acts like a wannabe gangster. He walks the halls with his chest out, chin low, staring people down like he’s guarding the neighborhood. Says stuff like, “People don’t want beef with me.” Bro, you flinched when the printer turned on.

But the funniest part? His friends all look just like him. Same haircut, same shoes, same bag of Hot Cheetos. They move in a group like a flock of identical NPCs. And their greeting is always the same. “What’s up, pa?” echoing down the hallway like it’s a ritual. They’re not scary. They’re just a copy-and-paste Edgar squad.
