# Ý TƯỞNG ĐỢT 1 — theo khung chuẩn (hook A/C + kết "your friend") — CHỜ DUYỆT
Đồ vật không trùng 14 mẫu; mọi bước phát minh có thật.

## ★ Đề xuất 1 — Cần câu (fishing rod) · hook A "rod"
Bước thật: dây + mồi → lưỡi câu xương → khoen dẫn dây trên cần → máy cuốn dây (reel) → cơ cấu "drag" nhả dây.
> You love your rod, but nothing ever bites. So you, being a genius caveman, tie a line to a stick and dangle a worm in the river. Clever! But the fish steal your worm and swim off, so you carve a sharp bone hook. Except the line keeps snapping in your hands, so you run it through little rings along the rod. Much better. But your spare line tangles into a giant knot, so you wind it onto a spinning reel with a handle. Except your friend hooks a huge fish and gets dragged into the river, so you add a drag that lets the line slip before it breaks. Now your friend can't stop playing with your rod!

~112 từ ≈ 32 s. Gag hình: cá giật mồi, cục rối dây khổng lồ, bạn bị kéo trượt xuống sông. Bối cảnh: bờ sông đá + làng hang.

## Đề xuất 2 — Que diêm (matches) · hook A "wood"
Bước thật: cọ hai que → khoan cung (bow drill) → đá lửa + sắt + bùi nhùi → que tẩm phốt-pho quẹt được → diêm an toàn (mặt quẹt trên hộp).
> You've got wood, but you can't get it to light. So you, being a genius caveman, rub two sticks together until your arms burn. Genius! But it takes all night, so you spin the stick with a bow string. Except it's still too slow, so you strike flint on iron and catch the sparks in dry moss. Much better. But the wind keeps blowing your sparks away, so you dip a tiny stick in phosphorus that lights when you scrape it. Except your friend keeps setting his pocket on fire, so you move the striker onto the side of the box. Now your friend can light your wood anytime!

~118 từ ≈ 34 s. Gag hình: tay rã rời, túi quần bạn bốc khói. Bối cảnh: trong hang ban đêm (ánh lửa).

## Đề xuất 3 — Khoá kéo (zipper) · hook C "fly"
Bước thật: buộc dây → cúc → móc-khuyết → "clasp locker" (1893) → răng khớp + con trượt (1913).
> Your fly keeps flapping open in the wind. So you, being a genius caveman, tie it shut with a vine. Clever! But untying it takes forever when you really need to go, so you sew on a row of buttons. Except you've got fat fingers and miss half of them, so you swap them for little hooks. Much better. But the hooks snag on everything you own, so you shape tiny metal teeth that lock together and pull them shut with a slider. Except your friend zips himself in too fast and screams, so you add a flap behind the teeth. Now your friend loves using your zipper!

~112 từ ≈ 32 s. Gag hình: chạy vội, ngón tay to, bạn kẹt khoá kéo (giữ ở mức hài nhẹ).

## Đã có sẵn (05/11): E01 "the tent" (59 s, kết A), E02 "the window" (35 s, kết B) — có thể viết lại theo khung chuẩn nếu chọn.
