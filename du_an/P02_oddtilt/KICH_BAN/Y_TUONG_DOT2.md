But it falls apart and gets stuck in your ass hair, so you press plant fibers into thin sheets of paper. Except the sheets blow all over your cave, so you roll them around a stick. Much better. But you unroll half of it for one wipe, so you punch little holes to tear off squares. Except your fat-ass friend uses the whole fucking roll in one go, so you hang it on a holder right next to your seat. Now your friend's ass never itches again!
