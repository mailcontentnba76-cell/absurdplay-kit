> You want to watch your friend shower, but his bathroom window is way too high. So you tape two mirrors to a broomstick and peek over the sill. Except it's way too blurry, and your friend hangs a fucking curtain, so you add glass lenses that zoom right through the gap. Genius! Now when light hits the top mirror, it bounces down to the bottom one, launching his image straight into your eyeball. But the broomstick snaps when you lean your fat ass on it, so you build a sturdy metal tube. But on your next peek, the steam fogs everything up, so you add a little wiper on top. Except your little cousin borrows it, sees your grandma in the shower, and screams for a week, so you lock it in a box. Now you can watch your friend shower every day!
