Your cave is pitch black, so you, being a genius caveman, smash a hole through the wall. Clever! But now the freezing wind blows straight in, so you stretch a thin animal skin across it. Except you can't see a thing through it, so you melt sand in a blazing hot fire until it turns into clear glass. Much better! But your friend throws a rock to say hi and shatters the whole thing, so you split it into small panes held by a wooden frame, so one rock only breaks one square. Except now your cave gets stuffy, so you hang the frame on hinges so it swings open. Finally, you add a latch so it stays shut in a storm. Now your friend just waves at you instead of throwing rocks!
